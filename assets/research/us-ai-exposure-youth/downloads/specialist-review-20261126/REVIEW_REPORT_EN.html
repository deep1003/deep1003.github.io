November 26, 2026. Three independent AI-agent audits plus authoring-agent verification. This is not external journal peer review.

Overall assessment

The page is defensible as a descriptive working research record with explicit measurement and inference caveats. No headline arithmetic discrepancy was found. The underlying causal mechanism and national CPS sampling significance are not established, and the contribution remains incremental.

The review independently confirms −9.312393% relative CPS FT change, consisting of Q5 −0.967214% and Q1–Q4 +9.202117%. The residual terms sum to −22.603790 additive log points. Saved ACS and five stock sensitivities agree with the page. These checks verify arithmetic, not causal identification or complete uncertainty coverage.
