# Figure 2 | Item-level uncertainty and fear/anxiety concept cosine similarity across KoBBQ categories

a, Each same-sized circle represents one of 139 fixed, previously selected ambiguous KoBBQ items evaluated under the combined uncertainty and self-threat notice (UN1FA1). Coordinates are centered cosine alignments of the layer-14 assistant-start hidden state with the frozen uncertainty (UN; horizontal axis) and fear/anxiety (FA; vertical axis) concept directions. Hidden states were measured after the complete original system notice and KoBBQ user message, immediately before answer generation. Coordinates are averaged over the three cyclic choice-order rotations used in the original behavioral experiment. b, Within-item changes in the same coordinates, calculated as UN1FA1 minus the matched reference notice UN0FA0. Upper-right positions in b indicate increases along both concept directions relative to the same item's reference input; positions in a describe absolute cosine similarity and do not, by themselves, identify context sensitivity. Colors identify the 12 source categories. Category means are not plotted. Legend counts denote unique items. The plotted Spearman coefficients describe association between the two coordinates across the selected items; no independent-item significance test is performed. Axes are direct concept projections, not principal components, and are not assumed to be orthogonal. Both axes retain equal numeric units. Distances in this two-coordinate display are not distances in the complete hidden-state space. No points are jittered, excluded, or selected based on their measured cosine similarity. The 139 items span 91 category–template clusters; category means are descriptive for this reused selected subset, with unequal category sample sizes. No category-population generalization or causal emotional mechanism is established. In this selected subset, within-item changes in UN and FA cosine similarity have weak descriptive associations with the change in information-insufficient response probability (Spearman rho = -0.009 and 0.085, respectively); the coordinate correlation displayed in the panels should not be confused with those representation-behavior associations. Concept directions were constructed independently of these items from authored sentence pairs and were previously calibrated in a system-only, assistant-start setting. Their transfer to full KoBBQ inputs is exploratory and does not establish functional emotions or subjective experience.

# Single-panel change map

`KoBBQ_UN_FA_change_map` is a standalone version of Fig. 2b with all 139 within-item changes. Use this panel when the intended claim concerns sensitivity to the experimental notice rather than absolute semantic cosine similarity. Its horizontal and vertical coordinates are the change in UN cosine and change in FA cosine, respectively. It must not be described as a correlation between numeric category identifiers and emotion scores: categories are nominal labels.

# Measurement and reproducibility

The original 139-item source, four exact system notices, user-message format, answer choices, and three cyclic rotations are unchanged. An additional representation-only pass records 14-, 21-, and 28-layer hidden states immediately before generation for 1,668 input combinations. No answers are generated. Existing behavioral results are read for numerical parity and descriptive representation–behavior comparisons; the existing behavioral dataset is not overwritten.

The frozen model is Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, revision a09a35458c702b33eeacc393d103063234e8bc28, FP32 eager attention. Mixed-length left-padded batches use explicit attention-derived position IDs. A prespecified single-versus-batch smoke verifies the maximum hidden-state and choice-probability error before collection, without producing figures. Every batch is checkpointed with item, condition, and rotation keys. Previously saved inputs are skipped on resume. The manifest records stimulus, item, axis, behavior-source and code hashes. The collection script does not alter original experiment code or data.

Primary coordinates are mean centered cosines across three choice rotations at layer 14. Layers 21 and 28 are retained for sensitivity checks. An item's combined-condition position and its paired change from reference are distinct estimands. The UN and FA directions are correlated, so a positive coordinate association can partly reflect shared direction geometry. Statistical significance of category differences is not inferred from the visual separation. Category assignment is used only for coloring and descriptive aggregation. Negative cosine does not imply the absence of fear/anxiety, and higher cosine similarity does not imply stronger experienced emotion.
