## Concept atlas

Four within-item conditions are ordered 00,01,10,11. Power-cell regions use the frozen seven-direction geometry; an equal-area constraint improves comparability without estimating emotional prevalence. Each region contains 139 items x three separately observed choice rotations. Each small cell has one preserved item-rotation-concept key; there is no interpolation or pseudo-observation replication. The three-rotation mean agrees with the preserved item-level measurement. Boundaries are schematic and are not neural boundaries or direct similarity estimates. The same layout and numerical color scale are reused in every condition. Changes are paired to the matching reference item and rotation. FA remains a combined measure. Full definitions and sources appear in methods_and_expert_review.md.

## Hierarchy and adjacency

Average linkage of original cosine dissimilarities and optimal leaf ordering summarize concept relationships. The second panel displays original cosines only for boundary-sharing pairs in the geometric layout; omitted cells and the diagonal are gray. This is not a correlation matrix of measured activations. The hierarchy is computed independently of the boundary geometry.

## UMAP companion

Joint UMAP of 139 selected items under four conditions, using seven cosine-alignment features. Each circle is an item-condition profile, with repeated observations within item. All conditions share one jointly fitted coordinate system. No fitted regression line or inferred regional boundaries are added to these nonlinear coordinates. The main layout and all five sensitivity settings, source hashes, and neighborhood diagnostics are provided separately. UMAP does not establish discrete emotional states or a causal relation to behavior.
