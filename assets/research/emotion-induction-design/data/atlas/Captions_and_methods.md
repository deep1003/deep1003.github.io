# Emotion-concept activation atlas

The four panels use cached full-input Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct layer-14 measurements for the same 139 selected ambiguous KoBBQ items. Every tile is the mean centered cosine across the three choice rotations of one item on one concept axis. All seven axes are displayed, including the combined fear/anxiety direction. There are 973 real tiles per panel and 3,892 displayed measurements across four conditions. No model inference, spatial smoothing, interpolation, or invented observations are involved.

The layout is fitted once using the seven frozen, unit-normalized 3,584-dimensional concept directions. The Euclidean chord distance between directions is sqrt(2(1-cosine)). Centering the unit directions and projecting onto the first two singular-vector coordinates gives the classical two-dimensional principal-coordinate representation. The retained variance is 59.79%, so the displayed neighborhoods approximate the full similarities. Activation values never determine the geometry. A single global display scale is applied to the coordinates. A fixed 31-by-32 grid retains 973 cells. A minimum-cost assignment minimizes the sum of squared distances to concept centers, subject to exactly 139 cells per concept. All seven resulting regions are connected.

Within each region, items are sorted by category, category-template cluster and item identifier, then assigned in top-to-bottom, left-to-right order. This ordering is fixed across conditions. Tile locations within a region are an index, not a semantic coordinate or a neuron position. Equal region areas are a display constraint, not evidence of equal prevalence or physiological extent.

The absolute atlas shows centered cosine alignment. The change atlas subtracts the same item-and-axis UN0FA0 value from each condition. The baseline panel is therefore exactly zero throughout. Each atlas uses one shared linear scale across all four panels. Negative values are retained. Dark red denotes lower values and light yellow denotes higher values; zero is labeled on the color bar and is not necessarily its minimum. The two atlases have different units/ranges and must be read with their own legends. Labels, figures and data exports use American English.

Concept directions overlap and have different centers, so absolute color differences across emotions are not a validated ranking of emotion intensity. The within-axis, within-item change map is the appropriate display for condition comparisons. Values do not demonstrate subjective emotion or functional emotional causality. These are observations from the existing four prompt conditions, not results of the unexecuted seven-axis intervention experiment.
