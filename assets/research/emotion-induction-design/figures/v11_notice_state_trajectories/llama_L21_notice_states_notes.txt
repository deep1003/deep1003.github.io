Notes. The main Korean wording of each notice was presented without a user question to Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (layer 21, float32), with activations read at the final token of the assistant opening. States were projected into the span of eight unit-normalized raw emotion-minus-neutral directions, retaining their common component, without the registered neutral-component removal or centering. Two principal components retain 94.9% of the five-state variation. REF is translated to the origin. Emotion names are projected direction labels, not centroids; distance to a label does not measure emotion intensity. Equal-area rings encode full-dimensional projection changes from REF across all eight directions, using a within-model range of 0 to 2. Color strength represents absolute change; hatching indicates negative change. REF is a comparison zero, not an absence of emotion content. Arrows connect separate conditions, not consecutive time points. PCA bases and activation scales differ across models, so absolute coordinates and color strength cannot be directly compared. This is an exploratory representation visualization, not a test of moderation or causal mediation.
