Eight raw emotion-minus-neutral directions were unit normalised without neutral-PC removal or centering. Emotion names are uniformly rescaled projected direction labels, not story centroids or measured experience. REF is a comparison origin, not absence of emotion. Rings use full-dimensional changes along eight raw directions; equal-area sectors are not proportions and hatching marks negative changes. Dashed arrows join separate inputs rather than a time course. PCA bases, layer boundaries and ring ranges are model specific, so absolute coordinates and colour strength cannot be compared across models.
