# K reliability diagnosis

All source data are read-only. No K generation or expansion is authorized.

Raw cosine can be high because of common residual-stream components and is not sufficient evidence of emotion validity. Shared-T1 centering is a descriptive sensitivity because current T1 cannot independently occupy the original contiguous halves. The additional even/odd split is globally topic-disjoint and is not used to replace the primary result after looking at scores. Neutral-PC components are fitted on independent N50 and reused unchanged.

Subsample bands are descriptive percentiles, not formal confidence intervals. Spearman–Brown values approximate cosine as classical reliability, assume independent errors and stable composition, and do not justify expansion under engine/topic confounding. Geometry matrices are not classification confusion matrices.

Retention tables show exclusive reason precedence and overlapping flags. Sentence trimming is a mechanical last-terminal-punctuation rule, not a semantic human approval. Original text and all derived hashes are retained. T1 synonym coverage and human review remain pending.
