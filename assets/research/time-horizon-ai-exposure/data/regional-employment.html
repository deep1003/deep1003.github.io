Data used in the study

Korean Regional Employment Survey

Korean occupational employment panel, age/cohort comparisons, wages, and web demographic and education analyses.

Provider
Statistics Korea / National Data Office, MDIS
Observation / edition
April and October waves, 2013–2025; type B public-use national files with three-digit occupation codes. The matched Korea–US exercise uses April 2020–2025 and ages 16+. Main Korean employment analyses use ages 15+.
Access and collection
14 November 2026.
Date evidence
The first-half README records the acquisition date; delivered archive filenames and the second-half acquisition records also identify this date.

Definitions and coverage

Raw row counts include people outside the analytical employed-person samples. Repeated cross-sections are person-wave observations, not unique people. Wages use positive-pay wage earners aged 25–64. Weights, education completion rules and age restrictions depend on the analysis. Raw MDIS microdata are not redistributed.

Documentation checked against the study’s local source records on 18 November 2026. Dates above distinguish observation periods, original acquisition records and derived-file versions.
