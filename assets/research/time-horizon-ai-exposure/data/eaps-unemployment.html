Definitions and coverage

Linked unemployment uses unemployed people with a valid previous occupation, divided by current employed plus linked unemployed. First-time and unlinked jobseekers are excluded. It is not the official unemployment rate and must not be interpreted as an occupation-specific employment-to-population rate. Raw microdata are not redistributed.
