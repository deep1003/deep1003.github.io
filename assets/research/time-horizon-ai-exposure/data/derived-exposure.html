Definitions and coverage

The descriptive statistics are unweighted over task statements or occupations as named. They are not employment-weighted means. The paper uses E5 for its original high-exposure classification and BGE for content and duration in THAIE. The web demographic groups use fixed 2025 THAIE. No Korean respondent data or full manuscript is included in these pages.

Documentation checked against the study’s local source records on 18 November 2026. Dates above distinguish observation periods, original acquisition records and derived-file versions.

Interpretation in the revised manuscript

The labels “content exposure E” in the tables denote transferred Eloundou β time-saving ratings, not task–AI cosine similarity. The paper uses E5 for group selection and BGE for both THAIE inputs. Duration-only and alternative-weight specifications support the temporal distinction in the Korean high-exposure sample, but do not establish the superiority of logistic weighting. A model horizon is a capability scenario, not a workplace adoption rate.
