Definitions and coverage

The publisher describes 6,075 principal occupations. The extraction used in the paper contains 6,074 entries. The paper and the linkage-corrected explorer are distinct mapping versions; their mapped-task counts must not be interchanged.

Documentation checked against the study’s local source records on 18 November 2026. Dates above distinguish observation periods, original acquisition records and derived-file versions.
