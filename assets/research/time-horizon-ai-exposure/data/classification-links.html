Occupational classifications and crosswalks

Connect Korean dictionary entries to KSCO-8 occupations, and US task scores through SOC to Census occupation codes.

Provider
KEIS, Statistics Korea classification materials, and US Census Bureau
Observation / edition
Korean dictionary edition 2020, KSCO eighth-revision codes, and the 2018 Census occupation / SOC crosswalk used with 2020–2025 CPS.
Access and collection
Korean concordance preparation dated 14 November 2026; US crosswalk acquisition package dated 18 November 2026. Original per-file timestamps are not recorded for these links.
Date evidence
Korean concordance README, crosswalk files, source inventory and US download manifest.
Source
Korean survey codebooks and classifications · US Census occupation code lists

Definitions and coverage

One-to-many links are not individual worker transitions. The paper’s dictionary aggregation and the explorer’s linkage-corrected mapping are distinguished on the dictionary page. Changes in occupation systems and link coverage limit cross-country equivalence.
