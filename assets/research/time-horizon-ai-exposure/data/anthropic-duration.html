Definitions and coverage

Durations are model-generated estimates of human work time, not stopwatch measurements or AI runtime. The table contains 5896 nonmissing duration targets. Source reporting and release windows differ; a file date does not by itself establish a conversation observation window.

Documentation checked against the study’s local source records on 18 November 2026. Dates above distinguish observation periods, original acquisition records and derived-file versions.
