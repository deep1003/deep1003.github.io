Definitions and coverage

Eligibility requires positive occupational employment in all six years, at least 100 baseline April records on average, and at least 80% SOC-link coverage. Cross-validation uses the upper conventional-exposure quintile, fixed 2020–2022 weights and identical controls in each score comparison.

Documentation checked against the study’s local source records on 18 November 2026. Dates above distinguish observation periods, original acquisition records and derived-file versions.
