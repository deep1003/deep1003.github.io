Policy Commons curation protocol

Measuring country-level attention to AI, beyond formal government reports.

Site protocol 3.8 · release protocol 3.7 · revised 20 September 2026 · local augmented release v5.1

The current local augmented master contains 269,853 unique records. It preserves all 11,306 Golden Set v6 records and adds 258,547 locally collected AI documents. After live review and the no-web automatic pass, 187,338 records carry country information and the final analysis view contains 187,221 unique unit-weight document-country rows across 137 ISO3-normalised countries. The remaining country-unresolved population is 82,482 records, comprising 81,866 new local records and 616 Golden Set auxiliary or unresolved records. The automatic pass merged evidence for 3,002 records, promoted 2,202 to the country analysis view and retained 800 international, supranational or multinational-private cases for review.

Purpose and workflow

Measure AI-related issues, interests and policy activity attributable to countries. The exact organisation within a country is secondary. A producer, publisher, public commissioner or official sponsor can establish a national connection. Different institutional names from the same country do not by themselves invalidate attribution.

Keep AI relevance, AI centrality, document form and country evidence separate. The collection was built from AI-oriented Policy Commons searches, including matches within document text. Therefore, collection provenance is positive inclusion evidence. It does not prove that AI is the main topic. Process local inputs first, then additional iCloud inputs under the same rules. Resolve aliases to avoid counting the same file twice. Preserve original metadata, collection categories and decision histories.

Freeze inputs → parse → deduplicate → screen AI with a high-recall rule → classify whole documents → attribute countries → count → validate.

Pre-classification error register

Run these checks in order before releasing any new or additional dataset. Each check records the trigger, evidence inspected, decision, rule version and affected record IDs. A warning is not an automatic exclusion. Unresolved cases remain visible for review.

1. Collection and RIS–CSV pairing errors

Risk and observed exampleRequired prevention check
RIS omits page-only fields. Policy Commons detail pages showed Published in Canada for a Government of Yukon document and Published in Italy for an ARPAE document, although the RIS did not expose those values.Collect RIS and CSV for the same query, filters, sort order, page size and page. Preserve CSV country and topics; never interpret a blank RIS country as evidence that the country is unknown.
Row-order or title joins can attach metadata to the wrong document. Export order can change and titles can repeat.Join only normalised CSV.coi to namespaced RIS.ID, then confirm the URL artefact ID. Quarantine unmatched or conflicting keys. Do not join on row number, title alone or approximate title similarity.
CSV topics can be mistaken for author keywords.Keep policy_commons_topics, RIS KW and analyst labels in separate fields with field-level provenance.

2. Duplicate-resolution errors

Risk and observed exampleRequired prevention check
One original can have several Policy Commons IDs or appear in several search batches.Generate candidates from stable identifiers, canonical source URL, exact normalised title, publisher, year and substantive-content hash. Merge only with compatible strong evidence and retain every source membership.
False merge of editions, translations or annual reports. Similar titles do not prove identity.Compare date type, language, edition, issuing body and source content. Keep translations, substantive revisions and annual editions as separate linked records.
Repeated gazette extracts can duplicate the same notice.Link overlapping extracts and count a substantive notice once where common source text is verified; retain unresolved overlaps as flags.

3. AI relevance errors

Risk and observed exampleRequired prevention check
False negative from metadata-only screening. Visa's Response to Commerce Commission on Retail Payment System Act 2022 had no clear AI term in its title or summary, but Policy Commons matched artificial intelligence within the document text.For documented AI-search collections, absence from title, abstract or keywords is not Non-AI evidence. Retain as collection_provenance_only unless the original or full-text evidence establishes an alternative meaning.
False positive from ambiguous strings. Examples include avian influenza abbreviated AI, the substring air, biological neural networks, and isolated ML, GPT or intelligent-system language.Exclude only when an alternative meaning is positively established and no independent computational-AI evidence remains. Historical terms such as expert systems, knowledge-based systems and machine intelligence require computational context.
Provider topics or generated summaries can be over-interpreted.Use them as supporting evidence, not proof of AI centrality. Preserve ai_include, evidence status and centrality as separate variables.

4. Document-form errors

Risk and observed exampleRequired prevention check
Standalone logistical agendas were counted as policy documents. Protocol 3.5 found 190 cases, including Council Meeting Agenda - June 12, 2026, Central Highlands Council Agenda and Berkeley City Council Agenda & Rules Committee Meeting. Round 2 found 42 additional patterns such as Notice of General Council Meeting, European Parliament Agenda: March 16-22, 2026 and committee agendas whose word order escaped the original phrases.Use order-independent detection: the presence of agenda plus meeting, council, committee, board or parliament, and phrases such as notice of ... meeting. Exclude only standalone logistical records.
Over-broad agenda exclusion removed substantive bundles. Round 2 restored seven records, including LGA Ordinary General Meeting Agenda and Papers, Meeting Agenda and Reports, Agenda and Business Paper, and Meeting Agenda and Summary. The full-census audit then identified and Round 3 restored four remaining cases: Chief Officer Appointments Panel Meeting Agenda and Report, State Government Artificial Intelligence Advisory Council Meeting Agenda and Benchmarking Report, Workshop Agenda and Presentations, and Workshop Meeting Minutes and Agenda.Before exclusion, test singular and plural forms of report, paper, presentation, minute and summary, plus supporting documents or materials, business papers, strategy, assessment, recommendations, briefings, submissions, transcripts and official records. These bundle signals override the logistical-agenda rule. Re-audit records carrying an older exclusion label after every override change.
Standalone recruitment material was counted as a report. Corrected examples were Digital Fairness Specialist Job Profile for the Office of the Ombudsperson and EU Institutions Job and Traineeship Opportunities Newsletter.Exclude standalone job profiles, vacancy announcements and opportunity newsletters. Retain recruitment policy, compliance, evaluation and labour-market analysis.
Recruitment synonyms escaped the initial rule. Round 2 found Developer Position Description - Ministry of Health.Add position description and job description to the standalone recruitment triggers, while preserving policy, evaluation, compliance and labour-market exceptions.
Archival finding aids were treated as substantive policy documents. Round 2 found National Archives of Australia: Indexed List of Files and File Index for Access Management and Custody & Storage.Classify pure file lists, archival catalogues and finding aids as auxiliary records. Do not exclude a substantive report merely because an archive hosts it.
Mixed documents can be over-excluded. A policy report containing a vacancy notice, or a gazette containing several notices, is not equivalent to a standalone recruitment notice.Classify the whole document. Retain substantive mixed documents with component or boundary flags; do not let one embedded notice determine the entire genre.
Academic and media formats can enter through broad searches.Exclude standalone theses, scholarly articles, patents, event programmes, audio, video, images and tables from the policy-document view. Scanned reports remain documents.

5. Country-attribution errors

Risk and observed exampleRequired prevention check
Publication place can conflict with explicit issuer jurisdiction. Six Government of Kosovo records were initially assigned to Serbia.Preserve both fields, but let the explicit issuer jurisdiction control analysis_country. Assign these records to Kosovo (XKX) and log the conflict.
Public issuing libraries were treated as passive repositories. Corrected bodies were Alberta Legislature Library (4), New Zealand Parliamentary Library (3), and the US Institute of Museum and Library Services (4).Distinguish a national, parliamentary or government library body that issues public records from a host that only mirrors files. The former can support national attribution; the latter cannot.
A domestic commission was treated only as an international organisation. Two Canadian Commission for Unesco records lacked Canadian attribution.Recognise official national commissions and supported domestic branches as national entities. Do not apply that decision to the parent international organisation or an unsupported headquarters location.
Substring matching created extra countries. British Columbia produced 59 false United Kingdom links; New Zealand Productivity Commission produced seven false Australian links.Use boundary-aware aliases and longest-specific-name matching. Regression-test that British Columbia maps only to Canada and New Zealand Productivity Commission only to New Zealand unless independent document evidence names another analytical country.
Supranational services can be converted into a host country. Community Research and Development Information Service is an EU service, not a Belgian national issuer.Keep EU and international organisations in a separate institutional class. Assign a country only for a supported national office, commission, policy jurisdiction or substantive country-specific target.
Published in can identify an international organisation's production location rather than a national issuer. A 100-record live-page audit found 11 EU or international-organisation records whose values included Belgium, Malta, Netherlands, Romania, Switzerland, Thailand and [New York] : UN.Store the value as published_in_country, inspect author, creator, source, rights and original-document branding, and classify the issuer separately. Do not count the host country when the issuer is the EU, European Commission, Europol, WIPO, UNESCO, the United Nations or another supranational body. A verified national commission, local office or country-specific policy mandate remains eligible under its own evidence.
Global professional firms can be assigned from brand or headquarters alone. KPMG, BCG and Deloitte operate through multiple national entities.Require a national office, commissioning relationship or country-focused report. A global brand and headquarters location alone are insufficient.
Language and multilingual publication can create unsupported countries.Use language as supporting evidence, particularly for distinctive national languages, but not as a unique identifier by itself. Multilingual does not mean multinational.

6. Counting and validation errors

Risk and observed exampleRequired prevention check
Two institutions from one country can be counted twice, while a genuine two-country document can be fractionally weighted.Create unique (record_id, analysis_country) rows. Give each supported country weight 1; collapse repeated institutions within the same country.
A deterministic rule check can be reported as semantic accuracy. Round 1 passed 1,500/1,500, Round 2 passed 2,000/2,000, and Round 3 passed 2,500/2,500, with no record reused across rounds.Label this as protocol conformance only. Report each sampling frame, seed, overlap and Wilson interval separately. Estimate semantic accuracy only through blinded original-document adjudication.
A sampled correction rate can be confused with a census change rate.When a new rule is applied to every record, report the exact release-to-release correction count and denominator. The v1-to-v6 value is 251/11,306, or 2.2201%.

Mandatory regression gate for every added dataset

Before release, run the vectorised rules over the full census, then confirm: exact ID linkage; no title/order join; order-independent agenda and meeting-notice detection; singular and plural substantive-bundle overrides; recruitment synonyms; archival finding-aid exclusion without excluding hosted reports; metadata-negative AI retention; ambiguous-AI exclusions supported by positive evidence; Kosovo jurisdiction override; public-library and national-commission handling; British Columbia and New Zealand collision tests; supranational and global-firm safeguards; unique document-country pairs; unit weights; preserved unresolved cases; non-overlapping repeat samples where feasible; and a versioned correction log. Every newly discovered error pattern must add both a failing example and a counterexample that must remain included. A failed full-census regression blocks the next release until adjudication.

Paired RIS and CSV source collection

The source corpus is now being enriched with a CSV export for every RIS search page under the same module, year, partition, sort order and page number. CSV contributes provider-supplied publication-country and topic fields, while RIS retains abstracts, identifiers, keywords and richer citation metadata. Collection prioritises complete raw capture and does not spend time evaluating page-level overlap. Final enrichment later joins only records with matching CSV.coi and RIS.ID, checked against the Policy Commons artefact ID; unmatched records are skipped from the enriched output and retained in raw storage.

Read the full code-level reproducibility specification and source snapshot.

1. Freeze inputs

Record original/real paths, SHA-256, format, query/category, retrieval time and cloud hydration status. Preserve the exact search string when it is available. If the RIS export does not contain it, label the provenance as user-reported rather than reconstructing or inventing a query. Parse identical file contents once, preserving all collection memberships. Freeze code, environment, dictionaries, institution/country tables, sampling seed and analytical years (1990–2026). Retain older and undated records in the archive.

2. Parse and check

Export every search page in both RIS and CSV under the identical query, filters, sort order, page size and page number. Preserve both raw formats. Derive observation IDs from file hash and record ordinal. Preserve raw RIS and repeated fields; normalise matching copies only. Extract identifiers, URLs, title, abstract/summary, keywords, dates, publisher, authors, affiliations, language and type. Preserve Policy Commons ‘Matches within the text’ evidence when it was captured. Distinguish provider-generated summaries from original abstracts and label unknown summary provenance explicitly.

Join CSV coi to RIS policy_commons_id after normalising only whitespace, URL prefixes and case. Confirm agreement with the Policy Commons URL where available. Do not join by title, row order or page position. Retain unmatched and conflicting records for review. Store CSV country as published_in_country. Store CSV topics separately as policy_commons_topics, retaining the original semicolon-delimited string and a normalised repeatable list. Do not overwrite RIS KW keywords with topics.

Quarantine shifted fields and unrelated joins. A PDF containing multiple notices is instead a mixed_document: retain qualifying content, record boundaries where possible and do not attribute every component to one agency. Recover missing years from an imprint or publisher record; distinguish publication, revision and retrieval dates. Otherwise retain unresolved status.

3. Deduplicate

Generate candidates from raw-record hashes, all namespaced IDs, DOI, canonical source URLs, exact normalised title, publisher, year and long summary/content hashes. Different Policy Commons IDs can refer to the same original document. Merge automatically only on strong compatible evidence: identical raw records; a shared stable identifier plus matching title; or exact title, publisher, year and substantive summary. Verified common original-source URLs may override different Policy Commons IDs. Title similarity alone triggers review.

Check date type, language, edition and source content before merging. Keep translations, substantive revisions and annual editions separately linked. Select the cleanest compatible record, fill missing fields only, and preserve conflicting values, merge rules and field provenance. Audit both false merges and residual duplicates. Link overlapping gazette extracts so the same AI notice is not counted repeatedly; flag unresolved overlaps.

4. Screen AI broadly

AI inclusion was established at collection, before this classification stage. The current Golden Set did not start from an unrestricted Policy Commons population. It was exported from a deliberately AI-oriented query and restricted source universe. Policy Commons searches the indexed document body as well as visible metadata, so a record can be a genuine query result even when its exported title, summary and keywords contain no AI term. For this reason, downstream validation does not require every retained record to prove AI relevance again.

Documented collection query and filters for the current Golden Set

Search expression: Artificial Intelligence AND AI Governance AND AI Safety AND AI System AND AI Policy

  • Modules: World Governments; World Cities and Local Governments; Public Health and Social Care; N.A. State, Provincial, and Local Governments.
  • Content: Publications.
  • Publication type: Document.
  • Publisher type: Government.
  • Export: paired RIS and CSV pages with the query, filters, page size and page number preserved.

This documented query provenance is affirmative AI-inclusion evidence. It supports retention but does not claim that AI is the central topic of every document.

Policy Commons AI search vocabulary used across collection waves

  • Explicit AI: artificial intelligence, machine/deep/reinforcement learning, neural networks, natural language processing, computer vision, generative AI, large language models; 인공지능, 기계학습, 머신러닝, 人工智能, 人工智慧, 人工知能.
  • Governance and applications: AI governance, risk, system, policy, ethics, regulation, safety, technology, strategy and standards; substantive applications and societal impacts.
  • Historical or ambiguous: expert systems, knowledge-based systems, machine intelligence, intelligent systems, 지능형, 머신러닝, LLM, GPT and ML require computational or explicit AI context when used outside a documented AI-query collection.
  • Alternative meanings: avian influenza, artificial insemination, biological neural networks and similar meanings are Non-AI candidates only when no independent AI evidence remains.

Downstream exclusion is deliberately narrow. Do not filter a record merely because AI terms are absent from exported metadata, the AI discussion is incidental, or the original cannot be opened. Retain these records as collection_provenance_only. Filter as non_ai only when positive evidence clearly establishes an alternative non-AI meaning, a contaminated join, or a hit confined to navigation, references or URLs, and no independent AI evidence remains. Ambiguous cases stay included.

Use policy_commons_topics as provider-generated topical evidence. It can support AI centrality and domain analysis but does not replace collection provenance or original-document evidence. Preserve RIS keywords, CSV topics and analyst labels separately. Record ai_include, evidence status, scope, exclusion evidence, rule version and review date. Re-run only the narrow high-confidence Non-AI rules when new data are added; do not send metadata-negative records into routine AI re-adjudication.

6. Attribute countries inclusively

Accept a supported national connection without requiring perfect within-country issuer identification. Preserve the relationship used for attribution. Policy Commons CSV exports include a country field that is often absent from RIS. Use the CSV value as provider-supplied publication-country evidence, not as an automatic substitute for issuer or analytical country.

Resolve apparent country gaps with CSV. A blank RIS country usually reflects an export limitation, not missing source metadata. For unresolved or low-confidence records, download CSV for the same search page and merge by the exact Policy Commons ID. Use CSV country / ‘Published in’ first, then country-explicit Source or organisation evidence. Infer from language or text only if these fields remain unavailable.
Required RIS and CSV linkage
Join CSV.coi to RIS.policy_commons_id. Preserve published_in_country, issuer_country, target_country, policy_jurisdiction and analysis_country as separate fields. Record country_source and the exact evidence used. If CSV and institutional evidence conflict, retain both and require review.

Host-location safeguard for EU and international organisations

Published in records where a file was published, catalogued or associated with an organisation. It does not by itself establish whose national policy attention the document represents. Apply the following sequence whenever the publisher or source may be supranational:

  1. Preserve the raw Published in value as published_in_country.
  2. Inspect author, creator, publisher, source, rights statement, institutional logo and the original source domain.
  3. Set issuer_scope to national, subnational, EU_supranational or international_organisation.
  4. Populate issuer_country only for a supported national or subnational issuer. Do not infer it from headquarters or publication location.
  5. For an international organisation, create an analysis_country only when a national commission, local office, explicit policy jurisdiction or substantive country-specific mandate is demonstrated. Otherwise retain the record as non-national.

The counter-rule is equally important: an official national commission or demonstrably national office of an international organisation can support national attribution. The evidence must identify that national entity or policy jurisdiction; a city, headquarters address or Published in value alone is insufficient.

Explicit jurisdiction in an issuer name overrides a conflicting publication place. For example, Government of Kosovo is assigned to Kosovo rather than Serbia. National, parliamentary and government library bodies may be actual public issuers, not merely repositories. Official national commissions and domestic branches of international organisations may support national attribution, while supranational services remain separate. Regression checks must also prevent substring collisions such as British Columbia being interpreted as the United Kingdom and New Zealand Productivity Commission as Australia.

For KPMG, BCG, Deloitte and similar firms, use a supported national entity/office, commission or country-focused report. A global brand alone is insufficient; exact intra-country office identification is unnecessary once the national relationship is supported.

Preserve published_in_country for the Policy Commons CSV/detail-page value and issuer_country/CTRY for issuer evidence. Store producer, commissioner, sponsor, target-country and policy-jurisdiction links separately; their qualifying union forms analysis_country. Do not silently relabel publication place or every institutional relationship as issuance. Use ISO alpha-3 codes, evidence references and unresolved flags. Apply evidence in this order: matched CSV/detail-page value, explicit institutional jurisdiction, original-document evidence, then controlled inference. Language and generic place evidence remain supporting signals only.

7. Count document-country pairs

Maintain broad AI candidates, eligible country-interest documents, and auxiliary/unresolved records. Apply AI and genre rules, select supported national relationships, then retain unique (record_id, analysis_country) pairs with weight 1.

A document linked to Korea and the United States contributes one to each, without percentage shares. Two Korean institutions still contribute one to Korea. Report unique documents and document-country rows separately. Multilingual does not automatically mean multinational.

8. Validate and release

Freeze the sampling frame, rules and seed before inspection. Stratify by period, institution type and error-risk mechanism, including agenda/recruitment exclusions, explicit jurisdiction overrides, provider-country use, issuer fallback and unresolved or supranational cases. Give small high-risk strata a minimum allocation, allocate the remainder by population size, and retain N_h, n_h and N_h/n_h. For an AI-oriented Policy Commons collection, do not routinely re-adjudicate AI relevance: retain the collection-stage AI presumption and record only clear non-AI contamination encountered during review.

Required checks for this and future datasets

  1. Confirm exact RIS–CSV linkage from the normalised Policy Commons ID and artefact ID.
  2. Test standalone agenda and recruitment exclusions, including substantive-bundle exceptions.
  3. Test CSV country against issuer jurisdiction, especially Kosovo and other disputed or conflicting cases.
  4. Test public libraries and national commissions as potential issuers, while keeping true repositories and supranational services separate.
  5. Run named collision tests for British Columbia and New Zealand Productivity Commission, plus newly discovered collisions.
  6. Confirm unique (record_id, analysis_country) rows and unit weights.
  7. Record inaccessible evidence as unverifiable; do not count it as semantically correct.

Apply confirmed deterministic corrections to the entire census, not only the sample. Report the resulting census correction rate separately from sampled conformance. A sample that re-executes the same coded rules measures implementation consistency. It does not estimate independent semantic accuracy unless reviewers inspect original documents without relying on the production labels.

Reconcile additional iCloud inputs under the same rules, then create a new versioned release. Preserve manifests, configuration, executable code/environment, dictionaries, canonical records, source/field links, duplicate decisions, country evidence, analysis membership, validation decisions, stage counts and checksums. Physical deletion is separate from analytical exclusion.
