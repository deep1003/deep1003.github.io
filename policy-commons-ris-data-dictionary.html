Policy Commons RIS Data Dictionary

Field definitions and a reproducible ten-file audit sample from the Global Think Tanks module of the Policy Commons collection. The search query was artificial intelligence, the publication year was 2021, and the sample was drawn from the files available at the audit snapshot.

Interpretation. This page documents the RIS export as observed. It does not claim that the 2021 collection is complete. Field presence is calculated across 847 records contained in ten RIS files. Repeated identifiers are retained here because deduplication occurs after corpus collection.

Sampling design

Ten files were selected without replacement using Python's deterministic pseudorandom generator with seed 20260919. The sampling frame contained 318 valid RIS files at the snapshot. The individual files contained between 38 and 100 records, with a median of 94.5 records. All 847 sampled records had the RIS type WEB.

Attribute dictionary

Attribute IDs are the two-character tags used in the RIS export. Definitions for standard RIS fields follow their conventional use. The C1 to C8 fields are provider-supplied metadata slots whose meaning depends on the source record, so their definitions describe observed use rather than a universal schema.
